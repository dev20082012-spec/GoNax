# GoNax Model Specification: European Beech Calibrated Allometric Estimator (Zianis-Beech-2005)
Model Identifier: zianis-beech-2005-standard
Species: Fagus sylvatica (European Beech, Family: Fagaceae)
Category: Scientific Trained Allometric Model
Status: Active Production Model

## 1. Model Overview and Mathematical Formulation
The European Beech Allometric Estimator calculates aboveground dry biomass (AGB, in kg) using the bivariate empirical equation:
AGB (kg) = 0.0632 * (DBH ^ 1.984) * (H ^ 0.912)
where DBH is diameter at breast height in centimeters and H is tree height in meters.
The scaling reflects the heavy diffuse-porous wood and extensive lateral crown architecture of Fagus sylvatica.

## 2. Carbon and Conversion Factors
- Carbon Fraction: 0.485 (48.5% elemental carbon), calibrated from Thomas & Martin (2012) for European Fagaceae.
  Carbon (kg) = AGB (kg) * 0.485
- CO2 Equivalent Factor: 44.01 / 12.011 ≈ 3.6667 (IPCC 2019).
  CO2e (kg) = Carbon (kg) * 3.6667

## 3. Calibration Domain and Boundary Limits
- DBH Range: 10.0 cm to 120.0 cm
- Height Range: 6.0 m to 42.0 m
- Sample Size: N = 330 destructively harvested trees
- Geographic Scope: Central Europe (Germany, Austria, Czech Republic)
- Residual Standard Error (RSE): ±10.2%
- Mean Wood Density: 0.68 g/cm³ (SD: ±0.04 g/cm³)
Extrapolation beyond 120 cm DBH incurs an EXTRAPOLATION_WARNING due to canopy breakdown and branch rot in ancient beech trees.
