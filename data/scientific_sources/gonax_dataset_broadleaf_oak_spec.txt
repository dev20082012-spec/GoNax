# GoNax Dataset Documentation: European Broadleaf Biomass Calibration Database
Dataset Identifier: ds-quercus-robur-v1
Species: Quercus robur (Pedunculate Oak / European Oak)
Version: 1.2.0
Status: Verified Scientific Calibration Dataset

## 1. Dataset Overview and Origin
This database aggregates destructive harvest records for European Oak compiled across forestry research institutes in Western and Central Europe (Zianis et al. 2005, Silva Fennica Monographs 4).
Contributing institutions include:
- European Forest Institute (EFI)
- French National Institute for Agriculture, Food and Environment (INRAE)
- Forest Research (United Kingdom Forestry Commission)
- Forest Research Institute of Poland (IBL)

## 2. Sample Size and Demographic Distributions
- Sample Size: N = 284 destructively harvested trees.
- DBH Range: 10.0 cm to 140.0 cm.
- Height Range: 5.0 m to 38.0 m.
- Geographic Coverage: Temperate Europe (France, Germany, United Kingdom, Poland).
- Habitat Types: Lowland mixed deciduous woodlands, oak high forest stands, and traditional silvicultural sample compartments.

## 3. Destructive Harvest and Laboratory Standardization
1. Trees were felled and segmented into stem sections, major structural boughs, crown branch wood, and leaves.
2. Fresh weights were recorded in the field with load cells.
3. Representative disc samples from the base, mid-stem, and crown branches were oven-dried at 105°C to determine moisture content and dry-to-fresh weight ratios.
4. Total dry biomass was calculated by applying component moisture ratios to fresh field weights.
5. Basic wood density measurements averaged 0.67 g/cm³ across the cohort.
